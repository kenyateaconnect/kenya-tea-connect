About Kenya Tea Connect

Kenya Tea Connect is an independent tea sales representation initiative based in Kenya. We aim to facilitate professional introductions between international tea buyers and Kenyan tea suppliers, helping businesses explore potential sourcing opportunities and develop commercial relationships.

Whether you are a tea importer, wholesale distributor, beverage company or private-label brand, we welcome inquiries about Kenyan tea sourcing and potential supply partnerships.
